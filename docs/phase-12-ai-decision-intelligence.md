# Phase 12 — AI Decision Intelligence

## 12.0 Decision Intelligence Foundation
Create a modular decision layer that consumes validated research context without bypassing existing engines.

## 12.1 Decision Context Builder
Build a normalized decision context from technical, fundamental, news, corporate-action, risk and evidence modules.

## 12.2 Signal Fusion
Combine validated signals while preserving each signal's provenance, freshness and confidence.

## 12.3 Bull/Base/Bear Scenarios
Generate structured scenarios with assumptions, supporting evidence, invalidation conditions and risks.

## 12.4 Entry & Exit Decision Framework
Translate validated signals into explicit entry, hold, reduce and exit conditions. Never present a decision as guaranteed.

## 12.5 Risk & Position Intelligence
Surface risk factors, position-sizing inputs, stop-loss considerations and concentration warnings without placing trades.

## 12.6 Decision Explainability
Every recommendation must explain the evidence, assumptions, conflicting signals and missing information behind it.

## 12.7 Decision History & Review
Persist decision snapshots so users can compare prior reasoning with later outcomes.

## 12.8 Decision Quality Evaluation
Measure evidence coverage, confidence calibration, contradiction rate and outcome quality.

## 12.9 AI Decision Guardrails
Block unsupported claims, stale/conflicting evidence and false certainty. Clearly separate facts, analysis, scenarios and decisions.

## 12.10 AI Decision Command Center
Unify market context, research, scenarios, risks, explainability and decision history in one responsive workspace.

## Architecture rules
- Existing technical, fundamental, news, corporate-action and research engines remain authoritative.
- The decision layer consumes validated context; it does not directly call market providers.
- No guaranteed returns, certainty claims or fabricated data.
- Preserve evidence provenance and confidence throughout the decision pipeline.
- Keep the implementation modular, testable, responsive and dark-mode compatible.
