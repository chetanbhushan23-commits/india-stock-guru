# Phase 11 — AI Research Intelligence & Decision Support

Phase 11 builds on the existing evidence, market-data integrity, and AI Q&A layers. It does not replace or bypass existing engines.

## 11.0 Foundation
Unified research-intelligence contract for question context, evidence, data quality, confidence, and provenance.

## 11.1 Research Intent & Planning
Break complex questions into explicit research tasks, entities, timeframes, and required evidence.

## 11.2 Evidence Fusion
Combine technical, fundamental, news, corporate-action, market, and research evidence while preserving provenance.

## 11.3 Contradiction Detection
Detect conflicting values, sources, dates, and claims. Surface conflicts instead of silently choosing one.

## 11.4 Temporal Intelligence
Understand current vs historical evidence, event chronology, freshness, and point-in-time context.

## 11.5 Scenario Analysis
Generate evidence-backed bull/base/bear scenarios and clearly separate assumptions from observed facts.

## 11.6 Risk Intelligence
Identify market, company, valuation, liquidity, event, data-quality, and thesis risks with supporting evidence.

## 11.7 Decision Framework
Turn verified evidence into a structured research conclusion without presenting it as guaranteed investment advice.

## 11.8 Research Report Generator
Generate structured research reports with summary, evidence, assumptions, risks, confidence, sources, and timeline.

## 11.9 Research Quality Gate
Validate source coverage, evidence completeness, freshness, contradiction state, unsupported claims, and confidence before delivery.

## 11.10 AI Research Command Center
Unified workspace for research planning, evidence, scenarios, risks, reports, history, quality, and confidence.

## Architecture rules
- Existing market, technical, fundamental, news, corporate-action, and research engines remain authoritative data providers.
- AI must consume normalized evidence through existing service boundaries.
- Never invent unavailable market data, sources, prices, financial values, or corporate events.
- Every material conclusion should retain source/evidence provenance where available.
- Conflicting or stale evidence must be visible to the user.
- This phase provides decision support, not guaranteed returns or personalized financial advice.
